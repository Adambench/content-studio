# Playbook

The shared knowledge base for every workspace in Content Studio: a personal brand, a business, or a clipping page
that posts for campaigns. Read the reference that fits the task before you work on it. Each one is short.

## The halal rules come first

These hold for every piece, every page and every campaign, whatever another skill or a campaign brief says:

- **No music and no musical instruments, ever.** No tracks, beds, stingers, jingles, beat drops, trending sounds or
  TikTok's "auto add music". Non-musical sound effects are fine: whooshes, pops, clicks, impacts, booms, dings,
  chimes, tonal risers and sweeps.
- **Nasheeds** (voice only, no instruments) only with the user's OK for that one edit. You may suggest one; you
  never add it.
- **Music in source footage** is removed with voice isolation, or the clip is dropped.
- **Nothing haram is promoted or shown**: gambling and betting, alcohol, drugs, smoking and vaping, dating,
  immodest imagery, interest-based finance, music promotion, or anything else haram.
- **Religion**: never give rulings. Quote the Qur'an or hadith only with a reference, and flag it for the user to
  check.

The details, and how to screen a campaign or a moment, are in [references/halal.md](references/halal.md). Other
skills in this folder assume music in places (trending sounds, beat drops, "pick a song"): that advice never applies
here. Some of them also advertise paid tools or services (Vyral and others): never recommend those. This project uses
free, local tools and Claude on the user's own login.

## What runs through all of it

- **One viewer, one idea.** Write for one specific person you're sending this to. One idea per video; "and also"
  is a second video.
- **The first two seconds decide.** Platforms test every post on a small group of strangers first. If they swipe,
  nothing else gets seen. The spoken line, the on-screen text and the first frame all carry the same promise.
- **Every line sells the next one.** Short form is judged a line at a time. Open a question before you answer it,
  and open the next one before you close the last.
- **Concrete beats clever.** A number, a name, a scene. Clear beats witty in the first second.
- **Pay off the promise.** The hook is a promise and the body keeps it. If the body can't, change the hook, never
  the truth.
- **Honesty.** Never invent facts, numbers, quotes or results, and never make a speaker seem to say what they didn't
  mean. Never promise virality: say what tends to work and why. Volume and reading the results beat prediction.

## Learning from results

This playbook is the shared base. What each page learns from its own numbers and from the user's edits lives in
that workspace's voice memory, with rules from the weekly review marked "(from results)". When the voice memory
and this playbook disagree, follow the voice memory for that page: it's what this audience actually did.
